Frequently Asked Questions

What wildlife issues are most common in Prince George's County?

Raccoons in attics, squirrels in rooflines, and rodent activity in basements and crawlspaces are common concerns for local homeowners.

Do you remove raccoons from homes in Prince George's County?

Yes. We look for the source of entry and focus on removing the animal while preventing future access.

Can you help with attic wildlife in Prince George's County?

Yes. Attic wildlife problems often involve noise at night, damaged insulation, and signs of nesting or entry around the roofline.

Do you provide pest control in Prince George's County?

Yes. We treat common household pests and can assess moisture, foundation, or exterior conditions that may be contributing to the issue.

How do I know if an animal is still inside my home?

Signs can include recurring noises, new droppings, chewed insulation, or fresh activity around the same entry point after an inspection.
