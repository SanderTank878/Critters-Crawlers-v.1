Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about wildlife removal, pest control, animal exclusion, or protecting your home? Find answers to some of the most common questions we receive below. If you don't see your question answered, contact us and we'll be happy to help.

Wildlife Removal

What types of wildlife do you remove?

We handle nuisance wildlife commonly found in residential and commercial properties, including raccoons, squirrels, bats, opossums, and other animals entering attics, crawlspaces, sheds, and exterior structures.

Do you remove raccoons?

Yes. Raccoon removal is one of the most common wildlife services we provide, including humane trapping, exclusion, and cleanup of affected areas.

Do you remove squirrels?

Yes. We address squirrel activity in attics, soffits, roofs, and wall voids by removing the animal and sealing the access points that allowed entry.

Do you remove bats?

Bat issues often need a careful inspection and exclusion strategy. We evaluate the situation and work toward a humane, long-term solution for the property.

Do you remove animals from attics?

Yes. Attic wildlife problems are a common focus of our work, especially with raccoons, squirrels, and other animals that enter through roof lines or soffits.

Do you remove animals from crawlspaces?

Yes. Crawlspaces can attract rodents and other wildlife when moisture, gaps, or foundation openings are present. We inspect these areas and address the source of entry.

What should I do if I hear an animal in my attic?

Try to limit disturbances, avoid approaching the area, and call for an inspection. Identifying the entry point early can help prevent more damage to insulation, wiring, and roofing materials.

Will you remove the animal and seal the entry point?

Typically, yes. Our process focuses on removing the animal, identifying how it got in, and securing the entry point so the problem doesn't return.

Pest Control

What types of pests do you treat?

We treat common residential pests including ants, spiders, roaches, termites, fleas, bed bugs, and other insects that affect the comfort and safety of the home.

Do you treat mice and rats?

Yes. Rodent issues can affect attics, basements, crawlspaces, and wall voids. We evaluate entry points and the surrounding conditions to create a practical treatment plan.

Do you treat ants?

Yes. Ant issues often require identifying the nest location, entry points, and access to food or moisture before treatment can be effective.

Do you treat spiders?

Yes. We help reduce spider activity by addressing web-building areas, exterior entry points, and indoor harborage conditions.

Do you treat wasps and other stinging insects?

We can assess stinging insect activity and recommend treatment that is appropriate for the structure, nest location, and level of activity.

Do you offer recurring pest control?

Yes, recurring pest control can help maintain protection against seasonal pest activity and reduce the chance of re-infestation over time.

How often should my property be treated?

Treatment frequency depends on the pest issue, the property, and the surrounding conditions. We’ll recommend a plan that matches your specific needs.

Home & Property

How do animals get into attics?

Animals usually enter through gaps around roof lines, soffits, vents, eaves, or damaged fascia. Once inside, they may settle in insulation, wall cavities, or under roof decking.

How do I know if I have an animal in my attic?

Common signs include noise at night, scratching or thumping, droppings, damaged insulation, and a strong odor. We can inspect the area to confirm the problem.

How do I know if I have a rodent problem?

Droppings, gnaw marks, grease marks, nesting materials, and signs of activity in basements or crawlspaces often point to rodents inside the home.

Can animals damage insulation or wiring?

Yes. Wildlife and rodents can damage insulation, chew wiring, and create sanitation issues that worsen over time if they remain in the home.

Can wildlife damage my roof?

Animals can disturb shingles, fascia, vents, and rooflines while entering or nesting. Damaged areas may need inspection and repair to prevent further water intrusion.

Can you inspect my attic or crawlspace?

Yes. Inspections help us locate entry points, signs of animal activity, moisture issues, and other conditions that may be attracting pests or wildlife.
