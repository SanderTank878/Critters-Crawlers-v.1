Frequently Asked Questions

What wildlife issues are common in Fairfax City?

Attic wildlife, squirrel activity, and occasional rodent problems often appear in homes with gaps along the roofline or crawlspace perimeter.

Do you provide wildlife control in Fairfax City?

Yes. We inspect the property and address the entry point, activity, and conditions that are allowing the animal to stay.

Do you remove raccoons from homes in Fairfax City?

Yes. We evaluate the home for attic or roofline access and work toward a durable exclusion solution.

Do you treat pests in Fairfax City?

Yes. We help homeowners resolve common pest issues including insects and other problem species that impact comfort and property health.

How do I know if I need an inspection?

If you hear noises, see droppings, or notice damaged insulation or chewed materials, an inspection can help confirm the source.
