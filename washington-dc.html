Frequently Asked Questions

What wildlife problems are common in Washington, D.C.?

Raccoons, squirrels, rats, and occasional bat or bird issues are common in older homes, mixed-use properties, and buildings with roofline gaps or utility openings.

Do you provide wildlife removal in Washington, D.C.?

Yes. We inspect the property, identify how the animal is entering, and work toward a long-term solution that reduces repeat activity.

Do you remove raccoons from homes in Washington, D.C.?

Yes. We address raccoon activity in attics, rooflines, and exterior structures, then focus on sealing entry points to reduce return visits.

Can you help with pest control in Washington, D.C.?

Yes. We work with homeowners dealing with insects, rodents, and other pest issues in living spaces, basements, and crawlspaces.

How do I know if there is an animal in my attic?

Noise at night, droppings, disturbed insulation, and signs of chewing around roof vents are common indicators that wildlife may be inside.

How quickly can I schedule an inspection?

The timeline depends on the issue and current schedule, but we are available to assess active wildlife or pest concerns as promptly as possible.
