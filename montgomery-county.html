Frequently Asked Questions

What wildlife problems are common in Montgomery County?

Raccoons, squirrels, rats, and occasional bat activity are some of the most common issues in older homes and properties with roofline gaps or attic access points.

Do you provide raccoon removal in Montgomery County?

Yes. We inspect the roofline, soffits, and attic area to confirm the issue and then work toward a safe, long-term removal and exclusion plan.

Can you remove squirrels from attics in Montgomery County?

Yes. Squirrel activity in attics often includes noise, displaced insulation, and entry points near roof vents or fascia areas.

Do you provide pest control in Montgomery County?

Yes. We handle common pest issues such as ants, roaches, termites, and other insects that affect homes and crawlspaces.

How quickly can I schedule an inspection in Montgomery County?

The best first step is to request an inspection so we can assess the property and recommend a practical treatment plan.
