Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common wildlife and pest concerns from homeowners in our service areas.

Do you handle raccoons in the attic?

Yes. We provide humane wildlife removal, attic cleanup, and exclusion work to stop animals from returning.

How do I know if I have termites?

Signs can include mud tubes, damaged wood, hollow-sounding areas, and swarmers around your foundation or crawlspace.

Can you help with bugs in crawlspaces and basements?

Absolutely. We treat moisture-related pest issues, insect activity, and structural concerns in basements and crawlspaces.

Do you offer emergency service?

Yes. We offer emergency wildlife and pest calls for urgent problems that need a fast response.
